About the game

One tap. One block. One very tall tower.

Sky Stack is a pick-up-and-play stacking game. The controls fit in one sentence, but the sky has opinions: gusts push your block, the tower leans with every sloppy drop, and storms roll in just when you are getting comfortable.

🧱

Precision stacking

Overhang is trimmed away. Land within the perfect zone and the block snaps into place at full width, and perfect streaks multiply your score.

⭐

Stars are your economy

Every floor pays stars. Perfects, cloud anchors and storm floors pay more. Spend them in the Workshop and on new builders.

🛠️

Permanent progress

A wider foundation, steadier hands, windbreaks, ballast and a star charm: five upgrades that turn ten floors into fifty.

How it works

Three steps, every build.

1

Drop

Tap anywhere to let the swinging block fall. Prefer a dedicated control? Switch to the big drop button in Settings.

2

Stack & adapt

Watch the banners and the lean meter. The sky always warns you before a gust or a storm arrives.

3

Upgrade

Spend stars in the Workshop, unlock builders with their own block colours and climb higher next time.

Signature mechanics

Four systems that make every tower different.

These are not power-ups you pick up. They are the weather and physics of the sky, and they play off each other.

🌬️

Wind Gusts

A banner warns which way the next gust will blow. While it blows, the swinging block drifts and slides a little after it lands, so you drop early against the wind. Storm gusts are much stronger.

⚖️

Tower Lean

Every off-centre drop tilts the whole tower. The lean meter shows how close it is to giving way. Push past the limit and it topples, however wide your blocks are. Perfects pull it back toward centre.

☁️

Cloud Anchors

Now and then a cloud drifts across the top of the tower. Land a block while the cloud covers it and the tower anchors: the block grows back to near full width and the lean resets to zero.

⛈️

Storm Bands & Sky Lantern

From floor 10, a five-floor storm returns every twelve floors. The sky darkens, gusts get stronger and every floor pays double. Three perfects inside one storm light the Sky Lantern, and the next three blocks centre themselves.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection to play?

No. Sky Stack is fully offline. Upgrades, builders and records live on your device.

Can I buy stars?

No. There are no in-app purchases. Every star is earned by building.

My tower keeps toppling even though the blocks are wide.

That is the lean. Off-centre drops add up even when nothing gets trimmed much. Land perfects or anchor to a cloud to straighten it, and buy Ballast in the Workshop to raise the limit.

The wind makes me miss.

Watch the banner: it tells you the gust direction before it starts. Drop a little early on the side the wind is coming from, or upgrade Windbreak.

I switched phones. Where is my progress?

Progress is stored locally on the device. Restore the device from an iCloud or computer backup to bring it along.

Something crashed or looks wrong.

Write to support@skystack.space with your device model and iOS version, or use reportaproblem.apple.com.
